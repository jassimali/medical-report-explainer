Vitamins such as Vitamin D and Vitamin B12 play important roles in maintaining overall health. Vitamin D supports bone strength, immune function, and calcium absorption, and low levels are common, especially in people with limited sunlight exposure. Vitamin B12 is essential for nerve function and red blood cell production, and low levels may be associated with fatigue or neurological symptoms. Reference ranges for these vitamins vary by lab, and what is considered low or optimal depends on individual health factors. It is important to discuss your vitamin levels with your doctor to understand their significance for you.
