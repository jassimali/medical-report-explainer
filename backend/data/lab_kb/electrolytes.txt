Electrolytes such as sodium, potassium, and chloride help maintain fluid balance, 
nerve signaling, and muscle function. Levels can shift due to hydration status, medications, 
hormonal changes, or kidney function, and even small deviations may cause symptoms in some people. 
Because reference ranges differ slightly between laboratories and individual needs vary, 
interpreting electrolyte values requires considering the full clinical picture. Your doctor can explain
how your specific levels relate to your health and whether any adjustments are necessary.
