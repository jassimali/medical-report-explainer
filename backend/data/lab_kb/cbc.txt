Hemoglobin (Hb) is a protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen. 
Low hemoglobin is called anemia. Common symptoms of anemia can include fatigue, weakness, and shortness of breath. 
Typical adult reference ranges are often around 13–17 g/dL for men and 12–15 g/dL for women, but exact ranges may vary by lab.
Always discuss your specific result and any symptoms with your doctor.
