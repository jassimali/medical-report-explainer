Thyroid tests measure hormones that regulate metabolism, energy levels, and many other body functions. TSH is the main signal from the brain that tells the thyroid how much hormone to produce, while T3 and T4 are the actual thyroid hormones circulating in the blood. High TSH often suggests an underactive thyroid, whereas low TSH may point to an overactive thyroid, though interpretation depends on T3 and T4 levels as well. Ranges vary slightly by laboratory and individual factors. It is best to review your thyroid results with your doctor to understand how they relate to symptoms or treatment.
