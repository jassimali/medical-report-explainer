Liver function tests measure enzymes and substances that reflect how well the liver is working. ALT and AST are enzymes found in liver cells, and higher levels may occur when the liver is irritated or inflamed. ALP is another enzyme that can rise with issues involving the bile ducts or certain bone conditions, while bilirubin reflects how the body processes red blood cells. Mild variations in these markers are common and can be influenced by medications, diet, or temporary illness. Your doctor can help interpret what your specific pattern of liver results means for your health.
