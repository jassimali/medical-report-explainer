Blood sugar tests help assess how the body manages glucose for energy. Fasting glucose reflects your immediate blood sugar level after a period without eating, while HbA1c shows your average blood sugar control over the past two to three months. Higher values may suggest impaired glucose tolerance or diabetes, but interpretation depends on your overall health and the exact ranges used by the lab. Reviewing these results with your doctor can help clarify what they mean and whether any lifestyle changes or treatments are needed.
