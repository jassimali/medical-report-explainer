Kidney tests help show how well the kidneys are filtering waste from the blood. Creatinine and urea are waste products that may rise when kidney function decreases, while eGFR is an estimate of how effectively the kidneys are filtering overall. These values can vary with age, muscle mass, hydration, and certain medications, so small changes do not always indicate a serious problem. Understanding kidney results requires looking at trends and individual health factors, so discussing your specific values with your doctor is important.
